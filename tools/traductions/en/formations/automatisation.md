Many businesses waste time on messages, appointments, customer follow-up, sorting information and repetitive tasks. An automation specialist helps them organise these operations and get their systems running on their own. This course teaches you to use digital tools and artificial intelligence to do this: acquire the skill, practise it on practical cases, then know how to offer it to clients.

## What you will be able to build

- A system that replies to the customer straight away
- A system that collects information from prospects
- A system that automatically records customers in a CRM
- A system that sends follow-up and reminder messages
- A system that organises appointments and bookings
- A system that links different apps and platforms together

## On the programme

- What automation is and how it works
- Analyse how a project operates and spot the tasks to automate
- Connect tools and platforms together
- Build prospect capture and follow-up systems
- Automate messages, appointments, emails and data
- Integrate artificial intelligence into these systems
- Create chatbots and AI assistants suited to a project's needs
- Put together a portfolio with examples of your work
- Find the clients who need this service
- Talk to a client and understand their problem
- Present an offer, set a price and agree the project

## How it runs and how you are supported

All sessions are recorded: you can go back to them at a time that suits you.

A team guides you while you learn and put things into practice. If a client comes along and you do not know how to analyse their need or build their system, you go back to the team: they guide you step by step.

## What this skill is for

- Offer automation services to businesses
- Work freelance
- Grow your own project
- Set up your own agency later on
- Add automation to social media and advertising services

## Good to know

You do not need to know how to code or have extensive technical experience: the course starts from the basics and moves forward step by step. Results do, however, take showing up, practice and consistency: tools alone are not enough. No figures or income are promised: they depend on your practice, your time and your work.
