Artificial intelligence has found its way into almost every line of work: businesses, e-commerce, marketing, content creation, freelance work. This course is not about watching videos and piling up information: you learn to actually use the tools, for your work or your project.

## What you learn to do

- Save time in your everyday work
- Get results faster
- Produce professional-quality content, visuals and ads
- Move your business or your project forward

## On the programme

- Practical explanations, step by step
- The most in-demand AI tools today
- Exercises and hands-on practice with real cases
- Regular updates on new tools
- Support and follow-up from the team

## Who it is for

The course is also for people starting from scratch, with no experience at all: it begins with the basics and moves forward step by step. You progress at your own pace, depending on the time you have available.

## And afterwards

Once you have got to grips with the tools, you can put this skill to use in your job or your project, or turn it into a business in its own right.
