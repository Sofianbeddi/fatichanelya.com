Digital marketing is about understanding the right people, speaking to them with a clear message, earning their trust through content, then turning that interest into enquiries and real conversations with your project. This course is not a series of videos to watch: it is a path to understand, practise, build real campaigns and content, and know how to improve your results.

## What you will be able to do

- Define your market, your ideal customer and their real problem
- Formulate a clear message and an identity suited to the project
- Build an offer that shows your value and what sets you apart
- Choose the platforms that match your audience
- Create a balanced mix of educational, interactive and promotional content
- Measure your results: know what works and what needs to improve
- Map out a clear path, from content through to getting in touch or placing an order

## On the programme

- The basics of digital marketing, and the difference between marketing, content and advertising
- Define the ideal customer, understand their problems and what they want
- Study the market and competitors, spot opportunities
- Build a clear positioning, identity and message
- Write content: hook, value, storytelling and call to action
- Put together a content plan for Instagram, TikTok and Facebook
- Use images and short videos to build trust
- The basics of paid advertising: objective, audience, budget and analysis
- Create a landing page or a simple journey to capture prospects
- Measure reach, engagement, prospects and conversion rate
- Practical case studies on real projects, campaigns and content

## Support

A team guides you while you learn and put things into practice. If you get stuck defining your audience, building a content plan or analysing a campaign, you go back to them to practise and correct your method.

## What this skill is for

- Promote your products or services more effectively
- Work in digital marketing for a business or a project
- Offer content and campaign management services
- Grow your project, its reach and its prospects
- Set up a marketing agency or a content team later on

## Good to know

No previous experience is needed: the course starts with the basics. Results do, however, take showing up, practice and consistency. No number of clients or level of income is promised: they depend on your work, your time and your market.
