Marketing draws the attention of the right people and helps you understand what they need. Selling presents the solution to them clearly. Persuasion teaches you to build trust and respond to hesitation, without pressure or manipulation. This course brings all three together: a path to understand, practise, hold real sales conversations and build a method you can repeat.

## What you will be able to do

- Define your ideal customer and their real problem
- Formulate a clear message that draws attention
- Build an offer that has value and gives a real reason to buy
- Ask the right questions and listen carefully
- Explain a benefit instead of listing features
- Respond to objections about price, time and trust
- Ask for the buying decision in a natural, respectful way

## On the programme

- The basics of marketing, and the difference between marketing, advertising and sales
- Define the ideal customer, understand their problems and what they want
- Carry out a simple study of the market and competitors
- Build a strong, clear offer that shows what sets you apart
- Write messages: hook, value and call to action
- The stages of a sales call, from opening to close
- The art of asking questions, listening and building trust
- The principles of ethical persuasion: proof, clarity, credibility and genuine urgency
- Handle objections without arguing or pressuring the customer
- Follow-up after the call, so that no prospects are lost
- Exercises, role-plays and practical scenarios

## Support

A team guides you while you learn and put things into practice. If you get stuck formulating an offer, talking to a customer or answering an objection, you go back to them to practise and correct your method.

## What this skill is for

- Sell your products or services better
- Work in sales or marketing for a business
- Offer a closing or customer follow-up service
- Grow your project and increase your conversion rate
- Set up an agency or a sales team later on

## Good to know

No previous experience is needed: the course starts with the basics. The aim is not to recite a memorised script, but to understand the person in front of you and choose the words that meet their need. Results take showing up, practice and consistency; no figures or income are promised.
