Media buying is not just about clicking the button to run an ad. It is preparation, understanding an offer and an audience, testing, reading the data and making decisions to use a budget wisely. This course is a path to understand, practise, build real campaigns, analyse their results and acquire a method you can repeat from one project to the next.

## What you will be able to do

- Set a campaign's objective and the expected result
- Choose the audience and the ad message that suits it
- Prepare the offer, the page and the journey before launch
- Choose the platform and the campaign type
- Test images, videos, headlines and copy
- Read the metrics: CPM, CTR, CPC, CPA and ROAS
- Stop what is losing money, improve what is average, cautiously scale what works

## On the programme

- The basics of media buying and the place of advertising in a marketing plan
- Understand a project, its offer, its ideal customer and its buying journey
- Study the market, competitors and ads already running
- Check the offer and the landing page before spending the budget
- Build a creative strategy: hook, angle, copy and call to action
- Set up accounts and campaigns on Meta Ads, with the principles of TikTok Ads and Google Ads
- Choose the objective, budget, audience and placements
- Tracking and measurement: pixel, events and UTM, depending on the tools available
- Understand CPM, CTR, CPC, CPA, conversion rate and ROAS
- Test, optimise, retarget and scale campaigns with caution
- Case studies, reports, campaign analyses and real scenarios

## Support

A team guides you while you learn and put things into practice. If you get stuck analysing a campaign, preparing a report or choosing an optimisation, you go back to them to practise and correct your method.

## What this skill is for

- Manage advertising for your products or services in an organised way
- Work as a media buyer for a business or an agency
- Offer a campaign management and optimisation service
- Grow your project and measure your customer acquisition cost
- Set up an advertising agency or a performance team later on

## Good to know

No previous experience is needed: the course starts with the basics. Results take showing up, practice and consistency. No sales figures or ROAS are guaranteed: an ad's results depend on the offer, the market, the creative, the page, the budget and the execution.
