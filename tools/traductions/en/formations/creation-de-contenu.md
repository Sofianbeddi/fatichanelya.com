A complete course in content creation for social media: video editing, ad visuals and ad videos, and the method for turning it into a service and finding your own clients. It is more than a course: it is a path to learn, practise, then move on to real projects.

## On the programme

- Edit professional-quality videos, for content as well as for advertising
- Design ad visuals that hold attention
- Create content for Facebook, Instagram and TikTok
- Find your first client
- Talk to a client and agree a project
- Use artificial intelligence to save time and work more professionally

## How it runs

- More than 90 sessions spread over a year
- Two sessions a week
- All sessions are recorded and sent to you: watch them again whenever you like, whether you work or study alongside

## Support

The team remains available during the course and afterwards. If you land a client without knowing how to handle the project, they guide you step by step through to delivery.

They also help you choose the way of finding clients that suits you:

- through content
- through advertising
- through direct prospecting
- by building your page
