<p>You are hesitating to order because you do not know where I ship to, how long it takes, or what happens if the product does not suit you. Here are the answers, plainly stated.</p>

<h2>Where I deliver</h2>

<p>I deliver to Spain and to all other countries of the European Union, with GLS. For the Canary Islands or a European country outside the Union (United Kingdom, Switzerland…), customs duties and taxes may apply: write to me before ordering.</p>

<p>Is your country not listed? Write to me, and I will tell you whether it is possible and at what price.</p>

<h2>How much delivery costs</h2>

<p>Delivery costs depend on the destination country and the weight of the parcel: I confirm them to you by message, together with the total for your order.</p>

<p>The exact amount is shown to you before you confirm your order. No charges are added afterwards.</p>

<h2>How long it takes</h2>

<h3>Preparation</h3>

<p>I prepare your parcel and hand it over to the carrier within 1 to 3 working days of receiving payment.</p>

<p>I ship the products from my own stock, in Spain. This is not an automated warehouse: I prepare your parcel myself.</p>

<h3>Transit</h3>

<p>The transit time depends on the destination country: I confirm it to you together with the total for your order, before any payment.</p>

<p>These are estimated times, provided by the carrier. A peak in activity or a public holiday may extend them by a few days.</p>

<p>What is certain, however: <strong>your order is delivered to you no later than 30 days after you place it</strong>. If I cannot meet this, I will let you know, and you may cancel and receive a full refund.</p>

<h2>How to track my parcel</h2>

<p>I ship with GLS. As soon as your parcel leaves, I send you its tracking number by WhatsApp or by email: you can track it on the GLS website. If you are not at home, GLS may leave the parcel at a pick-up point (Parcel Shop) near you; you collect it there before the deadline shown in the tracking information.</p>

<p>And if you have a question about your parcel, write to me. I am the one who replies.</p>

<h2>When your parcel arrives</h2>

<p>Two steps that will save you trouble:</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Look at the parcel before signing.</strong> If it is damaged, crushed or has been opened, note this on the delivery note and take a photograph before opening it.</li>
  <li><strong>Check the contents and the dates.</strong> If something is wrong, write to me with a photograph. With a photograph, I can resolve the situation straight away.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Returning a product: you have 14 days</h2>

<p>You may change your mind, without having to explain why. It is your right, and I honour it.</p>

<h3>How to do it</h3>

<ol>
  <li><strong>Let me know within 14 days of receiving</strong> your parcel, by WhatsApp or by email. A single message is enough: “I would like to return this product.” No complicated form. A model form is provided in the <a href="/conditions-generales-de-vente/">terms and conditions of sale</a> if you prefer to use it, but it is not compulsory.</li>
  <li><strong>Send the product back within 14 days</strong> of your message, to the address that I give you. Use a tracked service: until the parcel has arrived, you are responsible for it.</li>
  <li><strong>I refund you within 14 days</strong> of your request, using the same means of payment.</li>
</ol>

<h3>What I refund</h3>

<p>The price of the products <strong>and the standard delivery costs</strong> that you paid for the outbound delivery. If you chose express delivery, I refund the amount of the standard option.</p>

<p><strong>The cost of returning the product is borne by you</strong>, unless the product was damaged, out of date or non-conforming — in that case I pay, and you do not pay anything upfront.</p>

<p>I give you the return address by message when you notify me of your return.</p>
